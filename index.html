Knowledge infrastructure for specialized marketing AI.

ATLAS ENGINE is an early-stage project developing a multilingual system to acquire, process, evaluate, and retrieve marketing-relevant knowledge for grounded AI assistants.

Explore the architectureSee development progress

Purpose-built for marketing knowledge

Instead of admitting general web content indiscriminately, the planned platform applies marketing-relevance, provenance, quality, and rights-aware checks before material is eligible for the specialized knowledge base.

01 / ACQUIRE
Bulk and live sources

Ingest eligible public datasets and discover live sources through permission-aware crawlers and authorized integrations.

02 / CURATE
Quality and admissibility

Extract content, deduplicate, record source rights, and quarantine uncertain or non-marketing material.

03 / RETRIEVE
Grounded AI answers

Prepare multilingual search and retrieval pipelines to support a marketing-specialized AI agent.

Proposed platform architecture

Modular components designed to be independently tested and scaled as infrastructure becomes available.

Discovery + Job Control→Bulk Import / Live Crawl→Extraction + Processing→Marketing Quality Gate→Storage + Index→RAG Agent
Data infrastructure

Python processing workflows, durable job execution, and plans for PostgreSQL and Qdrant.

AI workloads

Multilingual embeddings, marketing relevance classification, evaluation, vLLM inference, and future fine-tuning.

Operational principles

Restart-safe operations, reproducible tests, clear audit logs, measured GPU costs, and modular deployment.
